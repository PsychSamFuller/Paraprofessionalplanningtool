Paraprofessional Support Matrix

A shared way for IEP teams to look at when and where a student needs adult support, based on what each person actually sees during the day.

  1. 1
    RateEach person rates the parts of the day they observe.
  2. 2
    PrintBring your report and rater code to the meeting.
  3. 3
    CombineThe case manager merges everyone’s ratings.
